ADAS calibration after windshield replacement in Tulsa

Modern glass carries the camera. If the windshield comes out, the camera has to be set again. Lane keep, forward collision, and adaptive cruise only work if that camera is looking where the factory pointed it.

Einstein does that work on the same visit as the glass — not as a later add-on, not at a second shop.

When it is needed

If the vehicle has a camera behind the glass, calibration is part of a complete replacement. Most vehicles from about 2016 on. If yours needs it, it is included in the appointment.

How the visit works

Glass first. Then the scan and calibration. You stay with the car or we text when it is done. Most visits are still forty-five to sixty minutes plus the calibration time the vehicle asks for.

Insurance

We do not file claims. You get a detailed invoice your carrier can reimburse.
